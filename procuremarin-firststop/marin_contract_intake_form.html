Step 4

Information Technology

Identify purchases that may need technology, data, security, or AI review.

1. Does this purchase involve any technology that may connect to County systems or handle County data, such as new software, cloud/SaaS, data sharing, integrations, or vendor security concerns?*
2. Does this purchase include any tools or features that use artificial intelligence (AI)?*
3. Will any personally identifiable information (PII) be shared with another organization as part of this work?*
Step 5

Risk

Identify activities that may need additional risk or insurance review.

1. Does the project involve public-facing work, field work, minors/vulnerable populations, transportation/facilities, unusual insurance or indemnity, or requests to waive/reduce insurance?*

Vulnerable populations are groups at high risk for poor health, exploitation, or harm due to social, economic, or physical disadvantages.

2. Is this a public works/construction project that is subject to prevailing wage and Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) requirements?*

Insurance

Confirm the vendor’s available insurance coverage and potential coverage needs.

1. All vendors must have General Liability Insurance. Do you have a certificate of insurance that is valid for at least the next six months?*
2. In addition to a certificate of insurance, Risk Management requires an “additional endorsement” naming the County of Marin. Do you have that document?*
3. Will this contractor be driving as part of their contract—and hence require auto insurance coverage?*
4. Will the contractor provide professional services—such as advice, consulting, design, or analysis—where a mistake could cause financial or operational harm to the County?*
5. Does this company have employees or is it a sole proprietorship?*
