      Step 3

      Human Resources

      Help identify potential staffing, employee, and labor considerations.

      1. Have you consulted with your Recruitment Analyst to explore staffing solutions?*
      3. Does this work include duties that are typically performed by an existing County job classification?*
      7. Is this an emergency or is there an exigent circumstance that requires this contract?*

      Emergency: a sudden, unforeseen threat to public health, safety, or welfare. Exigent circumstance: an urgent, uncontrollable situation requiring immediate action to prevent significant harm.

      9. Please include any attachments that provide details about the work being contracted out, such as a scope of work (SOW) or contract, if available. Including as much detail as possible up front will help expedite the review process and avoid delays.

      Step 4

      Information Technology

      Identify purchases that may need technology, data, security, or AI review.

      1. Does this purchase involve any technology that may connect to County systems?*
      2. Does this purchase include any tools or features that use artificial intelligence (AI)?*
      3. Will the vendor or product collect, see, store, or send data or information about individual people, such as clients, patients, residents, or employees?*
      Step 5

      Risk

      Identify activities that may need additional risk or insurance review.

      1. Does the contract involve any higher-risk or specialized activities? Check all that apply.*

      1 Vulnerable populations are groups at high risk for poor health, exploitation, or harm due to social, economic, or physical disadvantages.

      2. Is this a public works/construction project that is subject to prevailing wage and Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) requirements?*
      Step 6

      Insurance

      Confirm the vendor’s available insurance coverage and potential coverage needs.

      1. All vendors must have General Liability Insurance. Do you have a certificate of insurance that is valid for at least the next six months?*
      2. In addition to a certificate of insurance, Risk Management requires an ‘Additional Insured Endorsement’ (AIE) naming the County of Marin. Do you have that document?*
      3. Will this contractor be driving as part of their contract—and hence require auto insurance coverage?*
      4. Will the contractor provide professional services—such as advice, consulting, design, or analysis—where a mistake could cause financial or operational harm to the County?*
      5. Does this company have employees or is it a sole proprietorship?*
      Step 7

      Legal

      Identify contracts that may need County Counsel review.

      1. Are you using the standard Professional Services Contract (PSC) to execute this contract?*
      2. Does the vendor have any terms and conditions included on their forms, proposals, quotes, invoices, or other documents that would apply to this agreement? Also, has the vendor requested any changes or exceptions to the County’s standard PSC terms and conditions?*
